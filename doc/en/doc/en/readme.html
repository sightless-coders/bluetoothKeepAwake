Bluetooth Keep Awake

Keeps Bluetooth speakers and headphones awake by continuously playing a near-silent sound, so they do not disconnect or fall asleep during quiet moments.

Commands

  • NVDA+Shift+B: toggle keep-awake on or off.
  • NVDA+Shift+Alt+B: change the sound: quiet low tone (30 hertz), quiet high tone (19 kilohertz) or true silence.
  • NVDA+Control+Shift+B: change the tone volume.

Notes

  • The tones are meant to be inaudible. The low tone is below most small speakers' range; the high tone is above most adults' hearing. If you can hear one, switch to the other.
  • True digital silence does not keep every device awake. If your speaker still sleeps in true silence mode, use one of the tones.
  • The sound plays through the same audio device NVDA uses.
  • Playing continuously may use a little more headphone battery than usual.
  • NVDA also has a built-in setting (Audio category, "time in seconds to keep the audio device awake") that keeps the device awake briefly after NVDA speaks. This add-on is for keeping it awake all the time.
